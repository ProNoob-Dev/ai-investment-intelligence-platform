How Investment Intelligence makes a call

Three steps, all visible. Nothing about the decision is hidden from you.

  1. News in

    Every 30 minutes the platform pulls Indian business news (Economic Times, Moneycontrol, Livemint, Business Standard and more) and tags each article with the tickers it mentions.

  2. News sentiment, scored

    A rule-based engine reads every headline: weighted terms, intensifiers, negation, contrast and the size of the move. Every score traces back to the words that made it. No black box.

  3. Research call

    Recent sentiment counts most, history sets the pattern. Above +0.20 is a BUY, below −0.20 a SELL, anything between a HOLD, with the drivers and the headlines behind the call.
